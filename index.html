Day by day

A realistic five-day plan

Built around one base in Porto, with day trips rather than constant packing.

01Arrive

Land in Porto, wander Ribeira

Check in, then walk the riverfront at golden hour. Dinner somewhere small on Rua das Flores.

02Porto

Old town and port cellars

Morning in the Sé and Bolhão market. Cross to Gaia in the afternoon for two cellar tastings.

03Douro

Douro Valley by train

Early train to Pinhão. Quinta lunch, a short river cruise, and the slow route back.

04Minho

Braga and Guimarães

Bom Jesus in the morning, the medieval centre of Guimarães after lunch.

05Coast

Aveiro and Costa Nova

Canals in the morning, striped beach houses in the afternoon, back to Porto by evening.

Before you go

Three things worth knowing

🚆

Getting around

Regional trains cover Porto, Braga, Guimarães and Aveiro. Buy the reusable Andante card once and top it up.

🌤️

When to go

May, June and September give warm days without the August crowds. Vineyards are busiest at harvest.

💶

Budget & etiquette

Expect €60–90 per day mid-range. A small tip is normal, and a coffee at the counter costs about €1.
